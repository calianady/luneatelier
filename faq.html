Frequently Asked Questions

ORDERS

How do I place an order?

Simply browse our collections, select your desired item, choose your size, and click “Add to Cart.” Proceed to checkout to complete your purchase.

Can I modify or cancel my order after placing it?

To maintain fast processing times, we begin preparing your order as soon as it is confirmed. For modifications or cancellations, please contact us within 1 hour of placing your order. After this period, changes may not be guaranteed.

SHIPPING & DELIVERY

Where do you ship?

We offer worldwide shipping from our Paris studio and global fulfillment centers. Some restrictions may apply based on local regulations.

What are your shipping fees and delivery times?

    • France & EU: 2 to 5 business days
      International: 5 to 10 business days

    • Can I track my order?

    Yes. Once your order is shipped, you will receive a confirmation email with your tracking number and carrier link.

    SIZING AND FIT

    How do I know my size?

    Each product page includes a detailed Size Guide. If you're unsure, we recommend sizing up for a relaxed fit or contacting our style advisors at style@houseofmj.com.

    What if the item I ordered does not fit?

    We offer free exchanges for size issues. Please contact us within 14 days of receiving your order to initiate an exchange.

    RETURNS AND EXCHANGES

    What is your return policy?

    We accept returns within 14 days of delivery, provided that items are:

    • Unworn, unwashed, and in original condition
      Returned with all tags and packaging intact

    • To initiate a return, please email returns@houseofmj.com with your order number and reason for return.

    Are returns free?

    Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer unless the item is defective or an error was made on our part.

    Can I exchange an item?

    Yes, subject to stock availability. To request an exchange, follow the same steps as for a return and indicate your preferred replacement item.
