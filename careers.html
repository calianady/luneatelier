Careers House of MJ

Unfold your future in fashion. At House of MJ, we believe that fashion is not just worn—it’s lived, shaped, and reimagined by the people who create it. From our Paris atelier to digital studios around the world, we are a collective of visionaries, artisans, and innovators redefining contemporary couture. If you're ready to make your mark in a fast-paced, design-driven, and globally minded fashion house, we’d love to hear from you.

Why Work with Us?

Creative Freedom: We empower bold ideas and original perspectives at every level.

Parisian Excellence, Global Impact: Rooted in haute couture tradition, but always moving forward.

Sustainability & Purpose: We prioritize ethical sourcing, mindful design, and long-term change in the industry.

People-First Culture: We foster a workplace that values individuality, collaboration, and well-being.

Where We Are

Paris – HQ & Design Atelier

Seoul – Creative Liaison Office

Manila – Global E-Commerce Support

Remote – Select roles in marketing, design, and tech

How to Apply

To apply, send your CV, portfolio (if applicable), and a short cover letter to:

careers@houseofmj.com

Subject: [Position Title] – [Your Name]

Only shortlisted candidates will be contacted. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.
